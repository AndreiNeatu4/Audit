EU Accessibility Checker

Checks a web page against the European Accessibility Act — Directive (EU) 2019/882 (CELEX 32019L0882). Requirements are grouped under the directive's four principles — perceivable, operable, understandable, robust — and verified via the harmonised standard EN 301 549 → WCAG 2.1 Level A + AA.

Article 15 (Presumption of conformity): a website conforming to EN 301 549 is presumed to conform to the directive's accessibility requirements (Annex I).

Screen resolutions & DPI to test

A requirement is marked Fail if it fails at any selected resolution.

How to read these results

  • Pass An automated test found no problem for this requirement.
  • Fail An automated test detected a violation. Click the row for the exact elements.
  • Manual review This requirement can't be fully judged by a machine — a person must verify it.

Automated testing (engine: axe-core) can reliably verify roughly 30–50% of WCAG criteria. A clean automated report is not a guarantee of legal conformance: Article 15 of the directive grants only a presumption of conformity to sites that meet EN 301 549 in full, so the “Manual review” items still need human checking for compliance with Directive (EU) 2019/882 (CELEX 32019L0882).
